BeFood Business Analytics & Sales Prediction
Overview

This project focuses on analyzing large-scale food and beverage data collected from the BeFood platform to uncover customer behavior, restaurant trends, and sales performance. The project combines exploratory data analysis (EDA), data visualization, and machine learning techniques to support data-driven decision-making in the food delivery industry.

The workflow covers the complete analytics pipeline, including data collection, preprocessing, exploratory analysis, visualization, and predictive modeling.
---
Business Objectives

The project aims to answer several business-related questions:

Which food and beverage categories are the most popular?
How do customer preferences vary across districts in Ho Chi Minh City?
Which factors influence product sales performance?
What are the differences in restaurant performance between districts?
Can machine learning models effectively predict sales-related metrics?
